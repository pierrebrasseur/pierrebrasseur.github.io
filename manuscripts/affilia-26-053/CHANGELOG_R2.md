## Central change

The article was reorganized around a single object. R1 was organized around two —
a regional-institutional regime and a placeless "feminist social work" said to need
changing — which is what Reviewer 1 identified as the reason neither claim held the
paper.

**New object:** a *configuration of mandated care*, specified in the Belgian and French
medico-social regime, defined by four conditions:

1. access to a person's body or life is institutionally mandated rather than chosen;
2. that access is performed under a vocabulary of care, help, or treatment;
3. the authority to interpret what happens within it is held professionally;
4. the institution that would receive a complaint is the institution that arranges the access.

**New contribution claim:** forced intimacy is the structural medium binding the
normative system to the epistemic one, so that one arrangement produces exposure to
sexual violation *and* forecloses its nameability in the same movement. Epistemic
injustice therefore operates *before* disclosure, not only after it.

**New position for feminist social work:** not the problem, but the profession that
occupies the point of interpretation inside the configuration — institutionally, the
point at which a disclosure is converted into a non-event.
